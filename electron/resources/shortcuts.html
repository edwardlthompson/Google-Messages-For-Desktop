Keyboard shortcuts

  • Ctrl+F / Cmd+F — Find in page
  • Ctrl+P / Cmd+P — Print conversation
  • Ctrl++ / − / 0 — Zoom in, out, reset
  • Ctrl+Shift+L — Focus conversation list
  • Ctrl+Shift+M — Mute wrapper audio (incoming ring)
  • OS emoji: Win+. (Windows) or Control-Command-Space (macOS)

Google Messages for Web keeps its own shortcuts inside the page.
