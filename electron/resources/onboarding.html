Step 1 of 3 — Set messaging defaults

1 · Defaults 2 · Sign in 3 · Verify

Click each sample link and choose Google Messages as the handler. These clicks only register defaults — they will not send a text or require you to be signed in yet. Keyboard: Tab to a button, then Enter. Use Skip for now to leave.

Why these defaults?

Not contacts, mic, or SMS history access — only OS associations so sms:, tel:, and im: links open this app. Next you’ll sign in to Google Messages; after that, real links can start a new text.

This window minimizes when you open a sample so the OS chooser stays visible. Sample number +15555550100 is association-only (compose is suppressed during this step).
